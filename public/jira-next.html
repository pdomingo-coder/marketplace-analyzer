The read

  1. Do not publish an AI coding agent. Claude Agent (Atlassian) 5,301 → 6,710. Cursor 2,658 → 3,023. GitHub Copilot 2,538 → 2,868. That race is over for a new vendor.
  2. Atlassian’s own Cloud companion is the #1 grower. Data Center shops connecting to Cloud. You cannot out-Atlassian Atlassian on migration.
  3. What independent apps still grew: hide/lock fields, simple Gantt, free OKRs, license rotation, PII scans, backups, canned replies, timesheets, and one standout — a board you can share with a client who does not pay for Jira.
  4. Agile Pulse put seven apps in this 300 (Laya Board, two SprintPokers, two retros, export, AI workflow). Same playbook: one sharp visual job, Cloud/Forge, often free or cheap.
  5. Best first app if we are new on the Marketplace: a live, read-only client board (the Laya Board job). Widening to 300 still finds only that one client-share listing. SEO agencies already show work to clients. They will not buy those clients a Jira license.

Features the growers keep repeating

  1. They live inside Jira. No new website to log into.
  2. They do one job: hide a field, draw a Gantt, share a board, encrypt mail, count a JQL filter.
  3. Cloud / Forge. Data Center-only tools are not the story this week except Atlassian’s own Cloud companion.
  4. Free or cheap to start. Strategy Hub and Simple Gantt grew from ~70 installs. People try before they buy.
  5. AI agents are pulling more coding work into Jira, so admins then need fields, backups, and boards around that work.

    Questions

    Why not the raw top 300 by percent?

    A 1 → 2 jump is 100%. CogniRunner went 2 → 15 (650%). That is noise. We kept apps that already had 50 installs last snapshot so a percent means something. Then we took the top 300 of those.

    Why 12 days, not 7?

    The saved baseline is 2 Sep. Today’s pull is 14 Sep. That is the closest full week we have on disk. The week page may pick a nearer snapshot for browsing.

    What is Cloud companion?

    Atlassian’s own app to connect Data Center to Cloud. #1 this period. Not a product a new vendor can copy.

    Are installs companies or people?

    Jira Marketplace “installs” are sites (companies / Cloud instances), not Chrome-style user counts. One install can be a 10-person shop or a bank.

    Should we copy Cursor?

    No. Cursor, GitHub, and Atlassian are shipping coding agents into Jira. A first listing will not win that.

    Why a client board over Gantt?

    Gantt grew faster on a tiny base. Client-share is still only one listing in the 300 and matches agencies who already use GMB Everywhere. They need to show a client a live list without buying a seat.

    Do we need Jira experience to ship this?

    Yes, enough to build on Forge and pass Atlassian’s listing checks. The idea is small: one board, one share link, no seat. That is the point.
