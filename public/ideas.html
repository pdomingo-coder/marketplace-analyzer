The read

  1. Do not build another GBP audit. Phantom (20,000), Crush (10,000), and ProfilePro (10,000) already copy the audit job. GMB Everywhere is the one that scaled.
  2. Category Finder, Local Scan, Post Audit, and Teleport barely exist as standalone Chrome apps. Those features are already a reason to stay in the all-in-one, not spin-outs.
  3. Maps lead scrapers are the only huge adjacent market: Presto 400,000, MapsLeads 100,000. Flat this week. Low-to-mid stars. That is the Client Finder job, not an audit.
  4. Review-request links have a real listing (GatherUp 20,000, flat) plus a pile of QR/link clones under 6,000. AI review-reply clones sit around 300–1,000 users. GMB Everywhere already writes replies. A standalone reply app is a graveyard.
  5. True Maps geo-grid tools are tiny but loved: izyLocal has 1,000 users and 110 reviews at 4.9 stars. Fake-location extensions grew (one jumped 10,000 → 20,000). People want “rank from another city.” Teleport is the product version. Do not ship a GPS spoof.
  6. Website vs GBP (NAP, schema, on-page) is a homepage feature with only generic schema checkers (7,000–9,000) and NAP Hunter at 1,000. Thin on Chrome. Fine as a GMB Everywhere depth play, weak as a new store app.
  7. If we pick a next idea from this store: deepen GMB Everywhere, or treat Maps leads as a separate product. Do not launch a seventh GBP audit or an AI-reply extension.
