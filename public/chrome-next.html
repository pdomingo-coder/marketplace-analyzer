    Questions

    Why is the top 500 full of school apps?

    Districts push lockdown browsers and classroom filters onto millions of Chromebooks. Chrome-Stats then shows a huge user jump. That is IT policy, not a shopper picking a tool.

    What does 900% month growth mean here?

    Users now minus users a month ago, divided by users a month ago. Google stores users in buckets (1,000, 10,000, 100,000). Crossing a bucket looks like 900%. It is not nine times more people in a clean count.

    Is GMB Everywhere in this 500?

    No. It grew 200,000 → 300,000 (+50%). #500 on this list needed about +233%. It is still the GBP winner on this file.

    Should we copy Phantom?

    No. Phantom is a GBP audit that doubled this month to 20,000 users. You are already 300,000. Another audit is a crowded clone. Beat it with depth, not a new listing.

    Are Maps lead scrapers growing?

    No, not this month. Presto (400,000) and MapsLeads (100,000) are flat. Big market, no spike. That is still the Client Finder job if you want volume, not the growth job.

    What is the one small idea from this month?

    SEO agencies are installing “hide AI Overview” extensions (two listings in this 500). That proves they still live in the SERP. Meta Pixel Helper is a second adjacent option if they also run Facebook ads. Neither is a company. Put extras next to Teleport and Website vs GBP, not in a new app.
